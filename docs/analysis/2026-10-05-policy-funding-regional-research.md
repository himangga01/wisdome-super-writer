## Readiness terminology and realistic coverage

**R — research-fetch-ready candidate:** this research retrieved relevant official HTML metadata/content or an official attachment and identified a usable route. It means a bounded collector can be designed against observed material. It does **not** mean a product adapter, pagination, attachment extraction, rights/access policy, or recurring collection has passed acceptance.

**D — catalog/discovery only:** the authoritative organization/domain or navigation route is established, but useful collection behavior was not demonstrated sufficiently. Intro pages, inaccessible routes, image-only facts, and unsupported attachment formats must not silently become structured funding facts.

For an implementation registry with a boolean `collection_ready`, initialize these directory entries as **false until that adapter's acceptance is executed**. Keep research readiness in a separate field. This subtask established no production-ready regional adapter. A truthful initial scope is: *official nationwide source catalog, national official discovery where supported, and explicitly named regional/municipal adapters with separately reported live results*. It is not *all Korean city/county/district funding notices collected live*.

## Collection and elementary-language implications

These are design recommendations derived from the observed sources, not implemented behavior verified by this subtask.

| Observed issue | Required preservation / behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Source updates lag across official product pages and amendments | Keep snapshot time, source publication date, notice version, prior notice relation, and field-level provenance. Surface unresolved conflicts; do not replace older figures based on a secondary story. |
| One notice contains multiple programs and opening dates | Store program/variant-specific eligibility, application start, loan ceiling, repayment term, subsidy duration, and fee support. Yangsan's youth start must survive extraction and article generation. |
| “3% support” is easy to misread as “3% loan interest” | Explain subsidy as the municipality paying an agreed part of interest. Retain the bank rate as unknown unless provided. Use a clearly labeled hypothetical arithmetic example, never a personalized quote. |
| Guarantee, interest subsidy, and cash grant differ | Explain: borrowed principal still has to be repaid; the foundation supplies a guarantee subject to review; guarantee fees and bank interest are separate costs. A guarantee does not automatically cancel the borrower's debt. |
| Supply total and individual maximum are different | Keep program supply amount separate from per-business loan/guarantee ceiling. A published maximum is not an approval or promised payment. |
| Budget exhaustion / future date | Maintain `scheduled`, `open`, `closed`, `exhausted`, or `unknown` with evidence. Do not manufacture a remaining balance from an undated “support possible” label. |
| Image/HWP-only details | Produce metadata-only records with missing terms clearly marked. Use only approved extractors; preserve the existing closed legacy-HWP boundary. |
| Municipality is narrower than the foundation's province | Carry source jurisdiction, actual eligible municipality, and business-location rule separately. A Gyeongbuk foundation page can concern only Pohang or Yeongcheon. |
| Eligibility words vary by program | Avoid one global youth definition: retrieved Pohang and Yangsan use 19–39 but different business-age periods; retrieved Yeongcheon uses 19–45 and 24 months. |
| A source link is an application gateway | Display the official destination; collection does not entail filling forms, uploading personal documents, initiating guarantees, or applying for credit. |
